# Picture carousel
## Context
<!-- The situation that makes this requirement necessary, e.g. a user need, a law or standard, or a constraint from another system. -->
A Person can have several Pictures. Family members want to look at them one after another without leaving the Browsing Application.

## Requirement Statement
<!-- A bland statement of the requirement using the [RFC 2119](https://datatracker.ietf.org/doc/html/rfc2119) standardized vocabulary. The ruling SHALL be stated in an assertive voice. It SHALL NOT contain definitions or links, not even to other REQs; terms are defined in the glossary. -->
The Browsing Application SHALL show the Pictures of a selected Person inside the Browsing Application, one at a time, and SHALL let the user switch to the next and to the previous Picture.

## Justification
<!-- A sound reasoning why this requirement exists and not another one. Any revision of the REQ MUST take the justification into serious consideration. -->
Showing the Pictures inside the Browsing Application keeps the family member in the Family Tree, as the story player does for Recordings. Handing every Picture to another program opens a window per Picture and breaks the experience.
