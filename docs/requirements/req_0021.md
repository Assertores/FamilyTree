# Relative Generation
## Context
<!-- The situation that makes this requirement necessary, e.g. a user need, a law or standard, or a constraint from another system. -->
Family members want to know how many Generations lie between two Persons, e.g. to see that someone is a great-grandparent.

## Requirement Statement
<!-- A bland statement of the requirement using the [RFC 2119](https://datatracker.ietf.org/doc/html/rfc2119) standardized vocabulary. The ruling SHALL be stated in an assertive voice. It SHALL NOT contain definitions or links, not even to other REQs; terms are defined in the glossary. -->
The Library SHALL return the Generation of a Person relative to a given reference Person, as the Generation of the Person minus the Generation of the reference Person.

## Justification
<!-- A sound reasoning why this requirement exists and not another one. Any revision of the REQ MUST take the justification into serious consideration. -->
The Generation orders Persons from top to bottom in the Family Tree.

### Persons in different Family Graphs
Andreas Edmeier
> The Requirement Statement applies to any two Persons, also if they belong to different Family Graphs. Every Person has a Generation within their own Family Graph, so the difference is defined in either case. An earlier draft said so in a second sentence, which `/check-requirements` found to follow from the first.
