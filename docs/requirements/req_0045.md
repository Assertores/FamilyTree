# Assigning Generations
## Context
<!-- The situation that makes this requirement necessary, e.g. a user need, a law or standard, or a constraint from another system. -->
The Relations File only states Relation Types, not Generations. The Library places every Person into a Generation, also Persons whose Parents are not recorded, e.g. a Person who married into the family.

## Requirement Statement
<!-- A bland statement of the requirement using the [RFC 2119](https://datatracker.ietf.org/doc/html/rfc2119) standardized vocabulary. The ruling SHALL be stated in an assertive voice. It SHALL NOT contain definitions or links, not even to other REQs; terms are defined in the glossary. -->
The Library SHALL assign every Person a Generation such that every Child is in a lower Generation than each of its Parents, all Parents of a Child are in the same Generation, every Person is in the highest Generation these rules allow, and the highest Generation of every Family Graph is 0.

## Justification
<!-- A sound reasoning why this requirement exists and not another one. Any revision of the REQ MUST take the justification into serious consideration. -->
Generations counted from the top of each Family Graph give every Person a place in the Family Tree, also when the Data Set consists of several Family Graphs that are not connected to each other. Placing Parents of a Child in the same Generation keeps a Person who married into the family next to their Partner.
