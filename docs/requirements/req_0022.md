# Family Tree
## Context
<!-- The situation that makes this requirement necessary, e.g. a user need, a law or standard, or a constraint from another system. -->
Family members want to see a Person in the context of their family, as far as they choose.

## Requirement Statement
<!-- A bland statement of the requirement using the [RFC 2119](https://datatracker.ietf.org/doc/html/rfc2119) standardized vocabulary. The ruling SHALL be stated in an assertive voice. It SHALL NOT contain definitions or links, not even to other REQs; terms are defined in the glossary. -->
Given a Person and a Distance, the Library SHALL provide all Families of the Family Tree, each with all its members, and every member with its Generation relative to the given Person.

## Justification
<!-- A sound reasoning why this requirement exists and not another one. Any revision of the REQ MUST take the justification into serious consideration. -->
Computing the Family Tree in the Library lets every Application draw it in its own way without repeating the logic. Counting the Distance in Families instead of Relations keeps every Family whole: counting Relations between Persons tears Families apart, so that Children appear with only one of their Parents.
