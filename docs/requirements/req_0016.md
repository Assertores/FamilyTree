# Searching for Persons
## Context
<!-- The situation that makes this requirement necessary, e.g. a user need, a law or standard, or a constraint from another system. -->
Family members want to find a Person by what they know about them, e.g. a first name and a place of birth, without knowing the Person Id.

## Requirement Statement
<!-- A bland statement of the requirement using the [RFC 2119](https://datatracker.ietf.org/doc/html/rfc2119) standardized vocabulary. The ruling SHALL be stated in an assertive voice. It SHALL NOT contain definitions or links, not even to other REQs; terms are defined in the glossary. -->
Given a set of Details and a minimum number, the Library SHALL return the Person Ids of all Persons for which at least that number of the given Details match. A given Detail SHALL match if its text is contained in the Person's Detail; for a Detail with several values, e.g. the first names, if its text is contained in any one of them. An empty given Detail SHALL be treated as not given. A Detail that is missing in the Person File SHALL NOT match.

## Justification
<!-- A sound reasoning why this requirement exists and not another one. Any revision of the REQ MUST take the justification into serious consideration. -->
Searching by Details is how people remember others, and they often remember only part of a name or a place. Returning Person Ids instead of whole Persons avoids copying all Details of every match.
