# Entering that a Detail does not exist
## Context
<!-- The situation that makes this requirement necessary, e.g. a user need, a law or standard, or a constraint from another system. -->
The data collector often finds out that a Person had no title of nobility or no profession. Without a way to record it, an empty field in the form is saved as a missing Detail, and the finding is lost: a later reader cannot tell "had none" from "unknown".

## Requirement Statement
<!-- A bland statement of the requirement using the [RFC 2119](https://datatracker.ietf.org/doc/html/rfc2119) standardized vocabulary. The ruling SHALL be stated in an assertive voice. It SHALL NOT contain definitions or links, not even to other REQs; terms are defined in the glossary. -->
The Data Entry Tool SHALL let the user mark each Detail as known not to exist, and SHALL save a marked Detail as an empty text or an empty list. It SHALL leave a Detail that is neither filled in nor marked out of the Person File. When it opens a Person File, it SHALL show a Detail with an empty text or an empty list as marked.

## Justification
<!-- A sound reasoning why this requirement exists and not another one. Any revision of the REQ MUST take the justification into serious consideration. -->
The Library already tells an empty Detail apart from a missing one; the data collector, who only uses the form, needs a way to write it. Leaving out unmarked fields keeps "not entered" meaning missing, as before.
