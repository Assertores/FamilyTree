# Listening to stories in the Family Tree
## Context
<!-- The situation that makes this requirement necessary, e.g. a user need, a law or standard, or a constraint from another system. -->
The project started under the name Geschichtenstammbaum, a family tree of stories: family members go through the Family Tree by listening to the stories their ancestors tell in their Recordings.

## Requirement Statement
<!-- A bland statement of the requirement using the [RFC 2119](https://datatracker.ietf.org/doc/html/rfc2119) standardized vocabulary. The ruling SHALL be stated in an assertive voice. It SHALL NOT contain definitions or links, not even to other REQs; terms are defined in the glossary. -->
The Browsing Application SHALL play the Recording of a Person inside the Browsing Application, started from the Person in the Family Tree, and SHALL let the user pause and continue it.

## Justification
<!-- A sound reasoning why this requirement exists and not another one. Any revision of the REQ MUST take the justification into serious consideration. -->
Listening to the stories while moving through the Family Tree is the experience the project was started for. Handing the Recording to another program breaks it.
