# Replaceable Platform
## Context
<!-- The situation that makes this requirement necessary, e.g. a user need, a law or standard, or a constraint from another system. -->
The way an operating system lists folders, reads files, shows images and plays audio is the most volatile part of the project, and Applications may want to show Media in their own way.

## Requirement Statement
<!-- A bland statement of the requirement using the [RFC 2119](https://datatracker.ietf.org/doc/html/rfc2119) standardized vocabulary. The ruling SHALL be stated in an assertive voice. It SHALL NOT contain definitions or links, not even to other REQs; terms are defined in the glossary. -->
The Library SHALL access the operating system only through the Platform. An Application SHALL be able to replace each part of the Platform with its own implementation, and the Library SHALL use the Default Platform for every part the Application does not replace.

## Justification
<!-- A sound reasoning why this requirement exists and not another one. Any revision of the REQ MUST take the justification into serious consideration. -->
A replaceable Platform lets the project be adapted to future operating systems and storage media, even when only the compiled Library survives, while Applications that do not care keep working with the defaults.
