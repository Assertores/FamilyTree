# Free text Details
## Context
<!-- The situation that makes this requirement necessary, e.g. a user need, a law or standard, or a constraint from another system. -->
Dates in the existing data are written in different formats, and the template given to the data collector states that the format of a date does not matter. Places, gender and Relationships are written as the data collector knows them.

## Requirement Statement
<!-- A bland statement of the requirement using the [RFC 2119](https://datatracker.ietf.org/doc/html/rfc2119) standardized vocabulary. The ruling SHALL be stated in an assertive voice. It SHALL NOT contain definitions or links, not even to other REQs; terms are defined in the glossary. -->
The Library SHALL return every Detail, every Relationship and every date of a Relation exactly as it is written in the data file, with the escape sequences of the file format decoded. The Library SHALL NOT otherwise interpret, validate or convert them.

## Justification
<!-- A sound reasoning why this requirement exists and not another one. Any revision of the REQ MUST take the justification into serious consideration. -->
Historical information is often incomplete or vague, e.g. "around 1850". Any interpretation would lose information or reject valid data.
