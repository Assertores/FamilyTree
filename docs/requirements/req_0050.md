# Empty Details
## Context
<!-- The situation that makes this requirement necessary, e.g. a user need, a law or standard, or a constraint from another system. -->
A Person File can contain a key with an empty text or an empty list, e.g. `"TitleOfNobility": ""` or `"Profession": []`. This says that the Detail is known not to exist, e.g. that the Person had no title of nobility, which is different from a key that is missing because nobody knows or entered it.

## Requirement Statement
<!-- A bland statement of the requirement using the [RFC 2119](https://datatracker.ietf.org/doc/html/rfc2119) standardized vocabulary. The ruling SHALL be stated in an assertive voice. It SHALL NOT contain definitions or links, not even to other REQs; terms are defined in the glossary. -->
The Library SHALL NOT treat a Detail whose text or list in the Person File is empty as missing. It SHALL return such a Detail as a Detail that is present and empty.

## Justification
<!-- A sound reasoning why this requirement exists and not another one. Any revision of the REQ MUST take the justification into serious consideration. -->
Knowing that a Detail does not exist is information worth keeping: a reader can tell "had none" from "unknown", and the data collector does not research a Detail again that was already found not to exist.
