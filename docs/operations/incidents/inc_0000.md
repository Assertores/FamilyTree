# Example Incident
<!-- State: Open (the incident is running, or the record is being written), Resolved (the service behaves normally again), Reviewed (the post-mortem is done). Times are in UTC. -->
## Summary
<!-- Two or three sentences: what failed, for whom, for how long, and how it was mitigated. -->
The first versions of this example and of the [Example Object Page](../objects/example.md) described a made-up sign-in service, unlike every other example in this template, which describes the template itself. Both were rewritten on the day they were written.

## Impact
<!-- What users and the business noticed, in numbers where possible, e.g. failed requests, affected users, lost data, broken promises in the service level objectives. -->
- Two of the eight examples read differently from the others, until they were rewritten.
- No real record had been written from them yet.

## Detection
<!-- How the incident was noticed, e.g. an alert or a user report, how long that took, and whether the alert led to the right object page. -->
Noticed by reading, about two hours after it started. No check covers the tone of examples, so no alert could have fired.

## Root Cause
<!-- What made the failure possible, and the contributing factors. Blameless: the conditions, not the people. -->
- An incident record needs times, roles and a cause, and the template had never had an incident it could describe. A made-up service was the easiest way to fill those in.
- Contributing: no document says that examples describe the template itself; the other examples only show it.

## Chain Break
<!-- Where the chain from requirement to test to code broke, as classified in the [incident procedure](../incident_procedure.md#post-mortem), with the REQs and tests involved. -->
Missing requirement: the rule that examples describe the template existed only as a habit, not in writing.

## Consequences
<!-- The records, tests, work packages, and updates to object pages or the incident procedure that came out of the post-mortem. Shortcuts from hotfixes that stay become TDRs. -->
- [INC 0000](inc_0000.md) this example, rewritten to describe its own incident.
- [Example Object Page](../objects/example.md) rewritten, with this failure mode added.
- [CLAUDE.md](../../../CLAUDE.md) states that examples describe the template itself.
